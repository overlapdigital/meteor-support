METEOR / SUPPORT

A little help staying on time.

Contact Meteor support

For help, feedback or privacy enquiries, email meteorapp.support@gmail.com.

Please include your Meteor version, macOS version and a short description of the problem. If you attach a screenshot, hide private meeting titles, attendees and meeting links first. Never send passwords.

My meetings aren’t appearing

Check that your meetings appear in Apple Calendar on your Mac. Then open Meteor Settings and check calendar access and your selected calendars. You can manage permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

I’m not seeing reminders

Make sure Meteor is running in the menu bar, reminders are not paused and the relevant calendar is selected. Your Mac needs to be awake and unlocked, with events synced locally. Use Test alert in Settings to preview a reminder.

How do I manage notification banners?

Optional banners can be enabled or disabled in Meteor Settings. Their visibility also depends on System Settings → Notifications → Meteor. Full-screen reminders are separate from these banners.

How do I change or remove my information?

You can change or clear your optional name in Meteor Settings and revoke calendar permission in System Settings. For more about local records, uninstalling and privacy requests, see the privacy policy. Contact us if you need help removing local app data.

Who develops Meteor?
