# Meteor

**Don’t let your day get lost in space.**

Meteor helps you arrive at meetings on time, with reminders, countdowns and quick access to meeting links from your Mac’s menu bar.

## Get started

1. Open Meteor and allow calendar access when prompted.
2. Choose the calendars you want Meteor to watch in Settings.
3. Set your reminder timing and use **Test alert** to preview a reminder.

Meteor uses calendars already synced with your Mac. For Google, Microsoft or other accounts, first make sure your meetings appear in Apple Calendar.

## Your calendar stays on your Mac

Meteor reads your calendar to show reminders. It does not create, edit, delete or upload your calendar entries. No Meteor account is needed. Your optional name and preferences are stored locally.

For details about local storage, notifications, support emails and this website, read the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
